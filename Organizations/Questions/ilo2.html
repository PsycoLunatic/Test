Department of Labour Revision Assessment

Subject: International Labour Organization (ILO) Framework & History

Total Questions: 10 Time Left: 10:00
1. The Constitution of the International Labour Organization (ILO) was originally incorporated into which historic document?
Explanation: The ILO was established in 1919 and its Constitution formed Part XIII of the Treaty of Versailles.
2. What constitutes the unique "tripartite structure" of the ILO?
Explanation: The ILO is the only tripartite UN agency, bringing together Governments, Workers, and Employers (G + W + E).
3. Which of the following statements regarding India's relationship with the ILO is absolutely correct?
Explanation: India is a founding member (1919) and became a permanent member of the ILO Governing Body in 1922. The first ILO office in India opened in 1928.
4. Identify the current Director-General of the ILO (as of September 2026), who is also the first African to hold this position.
Explanation: Gilbert F. Houngbo from Togo is the 11th Director-General. He took office in October 2022.
5. Which major organ of the ILO is commonly referred to as the "Parliament of Labour"?
Explanation: The International Labour Conference (ILC) establishes international labour standards, meets annually, and is known as the "Parliament of Labour".
6. In which year did the ILO officially become a specialized agency of the United Nations?
Explanation: Although established in 1919 (before the UN), the ILO became the first specialized agency of the UN in 1946.
7. For what reason and in which year was the ILO awarded the Nobel Peace Prize?
Explanation: The ILO received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1969 on its 50th anniversary.
8. The famous principle "Labour is not a commodity" is a key feature of which important document?
Explanation: The Declaration of Philadelphia, adopted during WWII in 1944, established the principle that "Labour is not a commodity."
9. How many Member States does the ILO currently have?
Explanation: Currently, the ILO has an official figure of 187 Member States.
10. True or False context: International Workers' Day (May Day) on 1 May is celebrated worldwide to mark the founding anniversary of the ILO.
Explanation: 1 May is NOT the anniversary of the ILO. It is strongly connected to the 1886 struggle for an eight-hour working day in Chicago.
